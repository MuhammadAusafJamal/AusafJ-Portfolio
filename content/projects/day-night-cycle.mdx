## Problem

Lighting is the part of a 3D scene that's hardest to fake convincingly—a static day scene and a
static night scene are two separate art problems. Animating the transition between them is a
third, harder one.

## Approach

The sky shifts through day, sunset, and night on a loop, with shadows sweeping across the ground
as the sun moves and a moon taking over once it sets. Fireflies fade in after dark as a particle
system. A `lil-gui` panel exposes the cycle speed and lighting parameters live, which is what
turned this from "does it work" into "does it look right."

## What I'd change

The cycle is time-based, not tied to any real clock or season—a version that actually read the
viewer's local time would be a small change with a genuinely interesting payoff.
